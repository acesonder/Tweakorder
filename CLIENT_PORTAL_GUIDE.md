# Client Portal User Guide

## Welcome to the Tweakorder Client Portal

The Client Portal allows customers to create accounts, place orders, and track their order history.

## Getting Started

### 1. Registration

1. Visit the Client Portal at `/client-portal.html`
2. Click the **Register** tab
3. Fill in your information:
   - **First Name** and **Last Name**
   - **Date of Birth** - This is used to generate your unique username
   - **Security Question** - Choose a question only you know the answer to
   - **Security Answer** - Your answer to the security question
   - **Password** - Minimum 6 characters
   - **Confirm Password** - Re-enter your password

4. Click **Create Account**

Your username will be automatically generated based on your name and date of birth. For example:
- **Michael Brown** born **May 6, 1984** = Username: **MICBRO050684**

After successful registration, you'll be automatically logged in and redirected to your dashboard.

### 2. Login

1. Visit the Client Portal
2. Enter your **Username** (e.g., MICBRO050684)
3. Enter your **Password**
4. Click **Login**

### 3. Forgot Password

If you forget your password:

1. Click the **Forgot Password** tab
2. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Date of Birth**
3. Click **Continue**
4. Your username and security question will be displayed
5. Answer your security question
6. Enter and confirm your new password
7. Click **Reset Password**

## Using the Client Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see your dashboard with:

### Statistics Cards
- **Total Orders** - All orders you've placed
- **Active Orders** - Orders currently being processed
- **Completed Orders** - Fulfilled orders

### Order Tabs
- **All Orders** - View all your orders
- **Current Orders** - View orders that are in progress
- **Completed** - View fulfilled orders

### Order Information
Each order card shows:
- Order number
- Status (Processing, Accepted, Ready for Pickup, etc.)
- Items ordered with quantities
- Pickup or Dropoff type
- Scheduled time (if specified)
- Special instructions
- Creation and update timestamps

## Placing an Order

### Step 1: Select Products

1. Click **+ New Order** from your dashboard
2. Click on product widgets to add them to your order
3. Each click adds one quantity (click multiple times for more)

### Step 2: Review Your Order

Your selected items appear in the "Your Order" section with:
- Product name
- Quantity (use +/- buttons to adjust)
- Remove button

### Step 3: Choose Pickup or Dropoff

1. Select **Pickup** or **Dropoff**
2. Choose a location from the available options
3. Each location shows its address

### Step 4: Schedule (Optional)

- Use the date/time picker to schedule your order
- Leave blank for ASAP processing

### Step 5: Add Instructions (Optional)

Enter any special instructions such as:
- Delivery preferences
- Contact information
- Access codes or directions

### Step 6: Place Order

Click the **Place Order** button. Your order will be created with a status of "Processing" and staff will be notified.

## Order Status Meanings

- **Processing** - Your order has been received and is being reviewed
- **Accepted** - Staff has accepted your order and is preparing it
- **Waiting for Supplies** - Some items are not currently in stock
- **Waiting for Customer** - Staff needs to contact you
- **Further Information Needed** - Please check for messages from staff
- **Problems Unknown** - There's an issue being investigated
- **Ready for Pickup** - Your order is ready to be picked up
- **Ready for Dropoff** - Your order is ready for delivery
- **Awaiting Scheduled Time** - Your order will be processed at the scheduled time
- **Fulfilled** - Your order has been completed
- **Cancelled** - This order was cancelled

## Security & Privacy

- All passwords are securely hashed and cannot be retrieved
- You can only view your own orders
- Your account is protected by your password and security question
- Sessions are secured and time out after inactivity

## Tips

1. **Remember Your Username** - Write it down when you first register
2. **Use a Strong Password** - At least 6 characters, mix of letters and numbers
3. **Choose a Good Security Question** - Pick something only you would know
4. **Add Instructions** - Help staff fulfill your order correctly
5. **Check Status Regularly** - Log in to track your order progress

## Need Help?

If you have issues:
- Try the Forgot Password feature if you can't log in
- Contact staff through the main portal
- Check that you're using the correct username format (e.g., MICBRO050684)
